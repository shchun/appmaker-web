Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

Precipi.com ("we", "us") provides mobile applications and related services (collectively, "the Services"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information our Services collect and how it is used.

Apps and Services Covered

This policy covers the following Precipi.com apps and related web services. App-specific processing is described separately below; where it is more specific than the general description, the app-specific description applies.

  • Coffigo — an existing app covered by the general policy
  • Domado Timer — Android package: com.precipi.pomodoro, including the related web timer

Data Processing in Domado Timer

CategoryProcessing
Local storageTimer mode, work/break durations, progress, repeat and theme preferences, and completion counts are saved in browser storage or Android app storage. To prevent duplicate counts, Android prunes completion records to a seven-day window when recording a completion. There is no feature that synchronizes the complete saved timer state to our own server.
Analytics transmissionGoogle Analytics 4 receives events such as start, pause, reset, mode change, phase completion, repeat/theme changes, and opening help. Depending on the event, parameters include mode, configured work/break durations, remaining time, work/break phase, repeat preference, theme, and platform (web/Android). The Google tag may also process connection/device information and analytics identifiers.
Purpose and providerWe use Google Analytics 4 (Google tag), provided by Google LLC, for usage statistics and service improvement. Analytics information may be transmitted to and processed by Google.
PermissionsAndroid notifications, alarms, and vibration support the timer and phase-transition alerts. Denying notification permission limits displayed notifications. Alerts may be delayed if exact alarms are unavailable.
Deletion and collection controlsYou can remove local data by clearing the website's site data or the Android app's storage. App data may be backed up and restored according to Android backup/restore settings. Clearing local data does not delete analytics data already transmitted. The app currently has no dedicated analytics opt-out setting. Device advertising settings and browser opt-out add-ons are not guaranteed to block collection in the Android app. Contact the email below for analytics-data requests; what we can do depends on whether the relevant data can be identified.

Analytics data is handled according to the retention settings and deletion procedures of the Google Analytics property. Retention controls apply to user-level and event-level data; standard aggregated reports are not covered by the same automatic deletion setting. See the app-specific section above for local-data deletion instructions.
